01 / A community with purpose

More than a club.
A foundation for
your career.

Business Professionals of America is a Career and Technical Student Organization that connects education with professional experience. Through competitions, leadership development, service, and networking, students can explore their interests and put classroom skills into practice.

At Michigan State University, our established BPA chapter brings students together around a shared ambition: to grow into confident business and technology professionals. Our mission is to connect classroom learning with purposeful practice, encourage ethical leadership, and make professional growth a collaborative experience.

Our vision

A Spartan community where curiosity becomes capability, every member has room to contribute, and leadership begins with integrity and service.

Community & connection

Our goal: build an inclusive place for shared learning, peer support, and professional connections. We aim to encourage mentorship, service, and respectful collaboration.

Skills & professional development

Our goal: strengthen presentations, business writing, critical thinking, and technical ability. Connect academic learning with practical challenges and explore innovative business ideas.

Leadership & competition

Our goal: practice initiative, preparation, ethical judgment, and teamwork. Value disciplined effort and personal improvement alongside competitive achievement.

Confidence & career success

Our goal: support career exploration, interview preparation, time management, resilience, and lifelong learning. Help members explain their strengths and choose their next steps with confidence.

Our values: leadership, integrity, excellence, collaboration, growth, and service. The membership model and campus program below are proposed plans for this website draft; they are not adopted chapter policies or confirmed events.

The Spartan experience / Proposed program

A rhythm for
real progress.

A suggested semester built around consistent practice, meaningful connections, and room to explore. This is a planning model, not a live event calendar.

CONNECT / TWICE A MONTH

Chapter evenings

Proposed: first and third Wednesdays, 6:00–7:00 p.m. Eastern, during teaching weeks. A campus meeting room would be reserved before dates are announced; no room is currently booked.

PRACTICE / MONTHLY

Career skills studio

Rotate through résumé reviews, mock interviews, spreadsheet problem-solving, and short presentations. Each session would end with one practical takeaway.

PREPARE / BEFORE CONFERENCES

Competition practice groups

Suggested focus: finance, business operations, digital technology, and communication. Small peer groups would practice against current official event guidelines.

EXPLORE / EACH SEMESTER

Career conversations

Invite alumni and professionals to discuss career paths and workplace lessons. Speakers and university partners would be approached after chapter approval; none are confirmed.

CONTRIBUTE / EACH SEMESTER

Skills in service

Develop a volunteer project that puts business or technology skills to use for a community organization. Scope and partner selection would be agreed together.

REFLECT / SEMESTER CLOSE

Member showcase

Share a project, practice presentation, or personal learning milestone. Celebrate effort and improvement, and set a next-step goal.

Suggested meeting format

10 minutes of chapter updates, 30 minutes of hands-on practice, 15 minutes of feedback or small-group discussion, and 5 minutes to choose next steps.

Proposed conference preparation

Explore events early in the semester, confirm division eligibility with the advisor, create a preparation plan, and rehearse before state competition. Any national participation would depend on applicable qualification rules, chapter approval, and available funding.

06 / Make room for possibility

Your next chapter
starts with you.

Bring your curiosity, your ideas, and your willingness to learn. Our proposed membership model welcomes MSU students across majors, with room for both career exploration and competitive preparation.

  1. Tell us what interests you.Prepare an interest form with your goals and preferred pathway.
  2. Connect with the chapter.Attend an introductory meeting once the schedule is confirmed. Meet fellow students, explore pathways, and discuss membership with an officer or advisor.
  3. Choose your next step.Complete the chapter’s approved registration process, confirm any applicable dues, and choose a personal development goal. This draft form is not official registration.
Do I need to major in business?

Proposed policy: welcome currently enrolled MSU students from any major who are interested in business, technology, or professional growth. No prior competition experience would be required. Final eligibility must follow chapter and BPA requirements.

Do I need competition experience?

Proposed model: competition is optional. Members could focus on career development, community service, or leadership. Interested competitors would receive peer practice opportunities, with official event eligibility checked by the advisor.

What does membership cost?

Proposed budget: no additional local chapter dues, with applicable state and national dues charged separately once confirmed. Conference registration and travel would be separate. The chapter would explore fundraising and financial support; neither funding nor fee waivers are guaranteed. This is a proposal, not a published fee schedule.

What is the suggested time commitment?

Proposed baseline: two one-hour chapter meetings per month, plus an optional monthly workshop. Competitors could plan an additional one to two hours of practice each week near events. Actual expectations would be agreed by the chapter.

When would I join?

Proposed model: welcome interest throughout the academic year and offer onboarding at the start of each semester. Competition registration must follow official membership and event deadlines; expressing interest does not reserve a competition place.

How can I request accommodations?

Contact the chapter advisor and relevant conference organizer to discuss access needs and applicable deadlines. Local advisor contact details are awaiting confirmation.
